Electricity

You choose the retail electric provider. Most will ask for a copy of your lease.

  • Compare plans at powertochoose.org. Look at the 1,000 kWh price, and choose a term that fits your lease.
  • Give them the ESI ID above. It identifies the meter. It is not an account number. Ask them to read this service address back before you enroll.
  • Set the start date to your lease start date, a few business days before you arrive. A deposit or credit check is common.

Water, trash & recycling

The City of Corpus Christi bills these on one account: water, wastewater, trash, recycling, and stormwater. Do not open a separate trash account.

  • Start service online, or call 311. Outside Corpus Christi, call (361) 826-2489.
  • Have a signed lease that shows this address and your start date.
  • Trash (brown or green cart) is weekly. Recycling (blue cart) is every other week. Carts stay with the house. Check your pickup days. Missed pickup: call 311.
