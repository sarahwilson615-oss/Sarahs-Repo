## Build facts used

From the Graceland build order:

- **Size:** 8′ W × 16′ L (interior ~7′5″ × 15′5″ with 2×4 walls)
- **Wall 4 (16′):** centered 36″ 9-lite door (LHOS), two 24×36 windows, 7 transoms
- **Walls 1–3:** blank (no openings)
- **Exterior:** Steel Blue siding, Pearl trim, Snow roof
- **Floor:** 5/8″ treated plywood · **No loft**

## Must-haves mapped

1. **Bed nook** on an 8′ short wall — twin platform for minimum depth  
2. **Navy patterned floor** — shown in plans; use vinyl/LVP over plywood  
3. **Shiplap walls** — pearl/cream to echo exterior Pearl trim  
4. **L- or U-desk** on the opposite short wall — PC, Cricut, 3D printer  

Nice-to-haves (Layout A/C): compact seating + TV on solid Wall 2.
