## Decisions locked in

- **Twin bed** with curtain-track nook
- **No floating seating in the entry** — cozy seating sits flush on solid Wall 2
- **Mini-split** — indoor head high on Wall 2; outdoor condenser on that same exterior face
- Navy patterned floor + pearl/cream shiplap

## Build facts

- **Size:** 8′ W × 16′ L (interior ~7′5″ × 15′5″ with 2×4 walls)
- **Wall 4 (16′):** centered 36″ 9-lite door (LHOS), two 24×36 windows, 7 transoms
- **Walls 1–3:** blank (no openings) — Wall 2 is the mini-split wall
- **Exterior:** Steel Blue siding, Pearl trim, Snow roof
