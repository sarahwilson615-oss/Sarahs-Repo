Why this works

Curtained twin nook stays. Loveseat moves flush to solid Wall 2 so the door opens onto open floor — walk straight toward the desk. Mini-split mounts high on that same blank wall.

  • Bed: twin platform + ceiling curtain track (the nook you liked)
  • Seating: against Wall 2, facing a TV on the curtain-wall face — not in the entry
  • Mini-split: indoor head high on Wall 2 (no windows); outdoor unit on that exterior face
  • Desk: L on Wall 3 — PC, Cricut, 3D printer on the return

Finish direction

Tied to your Steel Blue / Pearl exterior and the must-haves you listed.

Floor Navy geometric vinyl plank or peel-and-stick tile (star / diamond / herringbone pattern). Waterproof over treated plywood.
Walls Horizontal shiplap (painted pearl/cream) over insulated + wrapped walls. Keep trim crisp white like the exterior Pearl.
Bed nook Twin platform with drawers; linen/blackout curtains on a ceiling track; reading sconce.
Mini-split Indoor head high on Wall 2 (blank long wall). Keep furniture low under it. Line set through Wall 2 to outdoor condenser.

Rough cut list (Layout A)

  • Navy patterned vinyl / LVP flooring — ~110–120 sq ft with waste
  • Shiplap + paint (pearl) — ~350–400 sq ft wall surface
  • Twin platform bed + twin mattress
  • Ceiling curtain track + linen/blackout panels
  • Compact loveseat (≤60″) against Wall 2 — or two lounge chairs
  • Wall-mount TV 32–43″ on the curtain-wall face (not blocking entry)
  • L-desk ~60–72″ main + 40–48″ return
  • Task lighting + outlet plan (computer, Cricut, 3D printer)
  • Mini-split (husband) — size for ~128 sq ft insulated shed; Wall 2 mount
