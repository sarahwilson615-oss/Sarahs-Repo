Why this works

Twin platform nook uses only ~3′6″ of length on Wall 1. The centered door lands in the lounge, and the L-desk claims Wall 3 with room for PC, Cricut, and a 3D printer on the return.

  • Bed: twin / twin-XL platform, wall-to-wall shelves or curtains as nook frame
  • Lounge: compact loveseat + small table; TV on solid Wall 2 (no glare from door windows)
  • Desk: L-shape — long run on Wall 3, return along Wall 4 or Wall 2
  • 3D printer: outer corner of desk zone — farthest from bed, near window for venting later

Layout B — U-Desk Studio

Tradeoff

U-desk gives dedicated runs for computer, Cricut, and 3D printer without sharing a surface — but the cozy zone shrinks to a single lounge chair.

  • Best if crafting is the primary use
  • Keep bed curtains closed when printing (noise/dust)
  • TV still works on Wall 2 opposite the chair

When to flip

Use this if one short end sits closer to a fence, AC compressor, or afternoon sun you want to avoid for sleeping. Functionally equal to Layout A.

  • Desk still gets a side window for daylight
  • Bed still spans the full 8′ width in a shallow nook

Finish direction

Tied to your Steel Blue / Pearl exterior and the must-haves you listed.

Floor Navy geometric vinyl plank or peel-and-stick tile (star / diamond / herringbone pattern). Waterproof over treated plywood.
Walls Horizontal shiplap (painted pearl/cream) over insulated + wrapped walls. Keep trim crisp white like the exterior Pearl.
Bed nook Platform with drawers; twin mattress; soft curtain on a ceiling track; optional reading sconce.
Desk zone Butcher-block or laminate L/U tops on cabinets or legs; separate surge strips for PC / Cricut / printer.

Rough cut list (Layout A)

  • Navy patterned vinyl / LVP flooring — ~110–120 sq ft with waste
  • Shiplap + paint (pearl) — ~350–400 sq ft wall surface
  • Twin platform bed + mattress (or daybed frame)
  • Ceiling curtain track + blackout/linen panels
  • Compact loveseat (≤60″) or two lounge chairs
  • Wall-mount TV 32–43″ on Wall 2
  • L-desk ~60–72″ main + 40–48″ return
  • Task lighting + outlet plan (computer, Cricut, 3D printer)
  • Mini split or portable AC (not on build order — plan early)

Assumptions baked into these plans

Confirm or override these and we can refine the drawings:

  • Twin / twin-XL mattress for the smallest nook depth (full needs ~4′6″+)
  • Layout A recommended unless you want max desk (B) or flipped ends (C)
  • TV is nice-to-have on the solid long wall; skip loveseat if you prefer a reading chair only
  • Door swings out (LHOS) — interior clearance at threshold is fine
